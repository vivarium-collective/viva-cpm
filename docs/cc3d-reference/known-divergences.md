## Triage & plan

- **🟢 resolved (D2, D8):** the `init_viral_load` IC over-seed (D8) collapsed the fig5 dose-response; fixing it (#63) restored the dose-response and, per the #64 re-evaluation, also closed the extracellular_virus band gap and the domain-dilution residual. Source-faithful, no tuning. (`g_vh` uninfected-uptake remains a deferred, optional refinement — see D2.)
- **🟢 irreducible (D1, D4, D5, D6, D10, D11):** these are source-faithful; closing them would require tuning (excluded) or geometry that's been ruled out (z=2 for D1). Track, don't "fix." D5 is a partial *success*.
- **⚪ accepted / deferred approximations (D7, D9, D12, D13):** documented, second-order or by-design. D12 (per-MCS field-update ordering) is already source-faithful for the chemotaxis read; a full secrete→flips→diffuse→infect restructure is deferred. D13 (periodic boundary) is a real source-divergence held back as too invasive for a piecemeal fix.
- **🟢 D3 resolved as source-faithful (audited this increment):** a full virus-field + infection audit against the re-fetched CC3D source found every constant (diffusion, decay, `g_hv`, `g_vi`) and the infection rule to be EXACT matches. The fig3b lag is therefore a gap vs the digitized *paper figure*, not the source *code*; per "source wins over paper" it is not a tunable bug. The only genuine source-code divergences the audit surfaced were the per-MCS field-update ordering (**D12** — current ordering already faithful for chemotaxis; a naive reorder was tried and reverted after it regressed NK/CD8 killing 9/10→~1/6) and the boundary condition (**D13, deferred**) — neither explains the lag magnitude.

**No open fixable-bug divergences remain.** The residual band gaps (D1 immune counts, D3 timing, D4 survivors) are source-faithful physics; closing them would require tuning (excluded) or an engine-level dimensional change (z=2, already shown not to move D1). The honest reproduction status: mechanistically complete and source-faithful, matching the CC3D **source** rather than the idealized paper **figures**.
