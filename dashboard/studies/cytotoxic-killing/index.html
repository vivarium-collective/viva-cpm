Claim

NK/CD8 chemotaxis up the (unchanged, Increment-6) chemokine field produces genuine spatial LOCALIZATION, robust across 5 tried seeds (NK mean distance change -20.37, CD8 -35.99, both negative in 5/5 seeds, against a near-isotropic lambda=0 control, NK -1.02/CD8 -0.54) with CD8 (2x NK's lambda) localizing more strongly than NK in every seed and on average -- matching the paper's Sec. 2.3 "CD8+ sensitivity twice that of NK cells." Separately, the contact-killing MECHANISM itself (`killing.contact_kill_rate`, the source's literal LOCAL surface-contact form, resist-DIRECT per discrepancy #7) is proven correct: a small close-contact scenario kills the one infected cell (1 -> 0 at update 22 of 45) with killing enabled vs steady at 1 with the identical seed and killing disabled. THIS STUDY DOES NOT CLAIM "NK/CD8 CLEAR THE INFECTION" AT REALISTIC SCALE -- at the scenario's own DEFAULT full scale (same 6-macrophage/6-NK/6-CD8, 60-update scenario the localization numbers above come from), NK/CD8 close much of the distance to the infection (NK ~81.6 -> ~57.8, CD8 ~81.6 -> ~39.8) but NEVER reach actual contact within the run budget, so `n_infected` is unchanged (stays at 1 for all 61 recorded steps, no kill fires). The killing CAPABILITY is demonstrated; end-to-end cytotoxic CLEARANCE at default/realistic scale is an OPEN Increment-9 field-magnitude-calibration item, the same root cause as the localization mechanism's own 100x engine-unit-scale correction, deliberately NOT tuned away here.

NK (`types.K`) and CD8+ T (`types.E`) cells are placed in the domain interior (the Task-7.1 three-cluster scenario: epithelial/infection patch | macrophage cluster | NK+CD8 cluster, every cluster >= `margin_sites` from every noflux wall) and wired to chemotax up the macrophage-released chemokine field (Increment 6, unchanged) via the engine's linear-lambda `World.set_chemotaxis` primitive, at the documented, non-per-seed-tuned `NK_CD8_CHEMOTAXIS_ENGINE_SCALE=100x` correction (Task 7.1 -- the literal source lambda is statistically undetectable at this engine's chemokine-field concentration scale). Over a 60-update run, tested across 5 seeds (5, 17, 23, 42, 100), both NK and CD8's mean distance to the infection centroid falls consistently when chemotaxis is on (NK mean change -20.37, CD8 -35.99, negative in 5/5 seeds each), against a near-isotropic lambda=0 control (NK -1.02, CD8 -0.54, no consistent sign) -- CD8 (2x NK's literal lambda) localizes more strongly than NK in every seed, matching the paper's Sec. 2.3 stated 2x-sensitivity ratio. Each update, `killing.contact_kill_rate` (the CC3D source's literal LOCAL surface-contact kill-rate form, applying `cell_resist` DIRECTLY per discrepancy #7, NK checked before CD8 per the source's ordering) draws a stochastic kill for every currently-infected cell from its NK/CD8 contact area; a small, deliberately close-contact scenario (2 macrophages/2 NK/2 CD8, tight margin/separation) confirms this mechanism actually clears the infected cell (1 -> 0 at update 22 of 45) once genuine lattice contact happens, vs steady at 1 with killing disabled, same seed. This study's central, deliberately UN-hidden finding: at the SAME scenario's own DEFAULT full scale (6 macrophages/6 NK/6 CD8, the scale the localization numbers above are measured at), NK/CD8 close much of the distance to the infection (NK ~81.6 -> ~57.8, CD8 ~81.6 -> ~39.8 over 60 updates) but NEVER reach actual contact within the run budget -- n_infected stays unchanged at 1 for all 61 recorded steps, no kill fires. The killing CAPABILITY is demonstrated and verified correct; end-to-end cytotoxic CLEARANCE at default/realistic scale is NOT -- this is the same root cause as the localization mechanism's own 100x engine-unit-scale correction (the chemokine field's raw concentration is far below what the source's literal parameters were calibrated for), compounded by the three-cluster scenario's extra cluster-separation distance, and is recorded as an open Increment-9 field-magnitude-calibration item rather than tuned away. Discrepancy #7 (resist-DIRECT), the deferred nearby-population killing term, haptotaxis, stubbed recruitment, and 2D-approximation geometry are all additional open items, not resolved here.

Result

The weak end-to-end result is NOT a separate bug — it is the SAME root cause Task 7.1 already flagged for localization: the chemokine field's raw concentration in this engine's units is ~2-3 orders of magnitude smaller than the virus field's at comparable distances, so the source's literal chemotaxis lambda (5000/10000) is statistically undetectable against thermal/adhesion noise at this field's scale, requiring the documented, non-per-seed-tuned `NK_CD8_CHEMOTAXIS_ENGINE_SCALE=100` correction just to produce a MEASURABLE on/off localization gap — itself still far short of closing the FULL distance to actual contact within a feasible step budget. This compounds with `build_cytotoxic_scenario_spec`'s three-cluster layout (epithelial/infection patch -> macrophage cluster -> NK/CD8 cluster): NK/CD8 must close TWO cluster-separations' worth of distance to physically reach an infected cell, not one. Both are Increment-9 field-magnitude-calibration items, recorded not tuned away (per the brief's explicit instruction not to smuggle a rescue via re-tuning): if this study widened `NK_CD8_CHEMOTAXIS_ENGINE_SCALE` or shrank `separation_sites` until the default scenario also cleared the infection, that would hide the real finding (the source's literal parameters don't calibrate against this engine's field-concentration units) behind a number that looks better than the mechanism's honest state.

Conclusion

LOCALIZATION MECHANISM VALIDATED (robust, 5/5 seeds, both NK and CD8, CD8 stronger per its 2x lambda); CONTACT-KILLING MECHANISM VALIDATED as a working, correctly-implemented primitive (close-contact test, 1 -> 0); END-TO-END CYTOTOXIC CLEARANCE AT DEFAULT/REALISTIC SCALE IS WEAK -- NOT DEMONSTRATED. NK/CD8 chemotaxing up the (Increment-6, unchanged) chemokine field genuinely localize to the infection, robustly across 5 seeds for both cell types, with CD8 localizing more strongly than NK (matching the paper's stated 2x sensitivity ratio). Separately, killing. contact_kill_rate (the CC3D source's literal LOCAL surface-contact kill-rate form, resist-DIRECT per discrepancy #7) is implemented and proven correct: a small close-contact scenario shows it removes an infected cell once NK/CD8 reach it. But at the SAME scenario's own default full scale (6 macrophages/6 NK/6 CD8, 60 updates, same seed as the close-contact test) NK/CD8 never reach actual contact within the run budget -- the infected count is unchanged. This study does NOT claim "NK/CD8 clear the infection": the killing CAPABILITY is demonstrated, but end-to-end clearance at realistic scale is an OPEN Increment-9 field-magnitude-calibration item, the same root cause as the localization mechanism's own 100x engine-unit-scale correction, deliberately reported as a gap rather than tuned away. Other open items, all recorded not resolved: discrepancy #7 (resist-DIRECT kill rate), the deferred nearby-population killing term (Increment 8), haptotaxis (not modeled), stubbed recruitment (a fixed population), 2D-approximation geometry, and the engine's linear (not the source's saturating/COM-scaled) chemotaxis form. No Figs 2/3A/3B/5/7 reproduction claim is made -- that verdict stays PENDING until Increment 9 (the capstone).

Plan & assumptions

Key assumptions

  • 100x CHEMOTAXIS ENGINE-UNIT SCALE — `NK_CD8_CHEMOTAXIS_ENGINE_SCALE=100.0` (Task 7.1), applied identically to NK and CD8's literal params.yaml lambda (preserving CD8's exact 2:1 ratio over NK), chosen from a one-time grid scan (50/200/500x) at a single seed then CONFIRMED (not re-tuned) across all 5 required seeds. The chemokine field's raw concentration is ~2-3 orders of magnitude smaller than the virus field's at comparable distances (chemokine diffuses ~87x faster, the same secreted mass spreads far more thinly), so the literal source lambda (5000/10000) is statistically undetectable against thermal/adhesion noise at this concentration scale -- a documented, non-per-seed-tuned engine-unit correction, an open Increment-9 calibration item.
  • DISCREPANCY #7 (resist-DIRECT) — `ContactKillingSteppable.step()` multiplies `kill_rate` by `cell_resist` DIRECTLY, not `(1 - cell_resist)`, unlike every other resistance consumer in this codebase (`transitions.infected_death_step`, `allee.*`, the secretion-scale gates). Implemented literally per the source, not "fixed" -- a cell with MORE local IFN is killed FASTER by contact NK/CD8, the opposite of every other resist-gated protective mechanism here. Left unresolved as recorded (possible intentional antigen-presentation effect, or a source inconsistency -- not adjudicated).
  • NEARBY-POPULATION killing term deferred — `ContactKillingSteppable` also applies a well-mixed, non-spatial "nearby" kill term (`params.yaml`'s `nk.killing.nearby_term_deferred`/ `cd8.killing.nearby_term_deferred`), independent of the LOCAL surface-contact term modeled here; requires Increment 8's population-scale ODE bookkeeping.
  • HAPTOTAXIS not modeled — only field-gradient chemotaxis (toward the chemokine field) drives NK/CD8 movement here; any adhesion/contact-gradient-driven directed-migration component is not implemented, an open Increment-9 item.
  • RECRUITMENT is stubbed — a FIXED NK/CD8 population (6 cells each) is placed at scenario build time; chemokine-driven inflow/outflow (`params.yaml`'s `nk.recruitment`/`cd8.recruitment`) is not modeled (requires the live immune-model ODE/SBML solver, Increment 8).
  • 2D-APPROXIMATION geometry — the same non-confluent, interior-placed 2D three-cluster patch as Increments 5/6, not the source's z=2 immune-layer-over-confluent-z=1-sheet geometry. Faithful z=2 geometry is deferred to Increment 9, and may place NK/CD8 closer to the infection than this study's layout does (see report.main_expert_question).
  • CHEMOTAXIS FUNCTIONAL-FORM gap — the source uses a saturating, per-cell-COM-scaled lambda (`chemotaxis_v_nk / (1 + concentration)`); the engine primitive used here (`World.set_chemotaxis`) is a fixed LINEAR-regime lambda per (field, type). The fidelity criterion tested here is the localization/killing BEHAVIOR, not the exact functional form -- an Increment-9 calibration item.
  • END-TO-END CLEARANCE not demonstrated at default scale — reported honestly as this study's central gap (finding F-04), not tuned away by widening `NK_CD8_CHEMOTAXIS_ENGINE_SCALE` further or shrinking `separation_sites` until the default scenario also clears the infection; doing so would hide the real finding (the source's literal parameters don't calibrate against this engine's field-concentration units at any scale tried so far) behind a number that looks better than the mechanism's honest state.
  • No chemokine-driven APC/global-ODE-coupled recruitment amplification, no global ODE compartment, and no Figs 2/3A/3B/5/7 reproduction claim exist yet -- all later increments.
