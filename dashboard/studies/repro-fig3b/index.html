Claim

`run.repro_fig3b` is implemented and wired correctly: it runs a seeded ensemble of `run_full_model` over the Fig-3B scenario, maps every observable onto `targets/fig3b.json`'s exact keys, ensemble-means via `bands.aggregate_replicas`, and evaluates via `bands.evaluate_study`, producing a well-formed `{"ensemble":..., "band_eval":{"passed": bool, ...}, "replicas":..., "cells_per_side":..., "steps":...}` dict (verified by `tests/test_influenza_full_model.py:: test_repro_fig3b_runs_and_evaluates_bands`). THIS STUDY DOES NOT CLAIM FIG-3B IS REPRODUCED. At the reduced scale this task's fast test uses (`replicas=2, cells_per_side=15, steps=20, seed0=0`), `band_eval['passed']` is False and 0 of the 12 fig3b observables are in-band at every checkpoint (per-observable n_in/8 and worst_miss reported in `report. key_metrics` above) -- expected given the ~5.4x smaller epithelial population and the ~0.09-simulated-day run duration vs. the target's 0.0-3.5-day checkpoint window, not a mechanism defect. The paper-scale 50-replica, 35x35-cell, ~3.5-day ensemble (Mac-mini Phase-B) is required before any `reproduced` verdict for Fig 3B, and `conclusion_verdicts. biological_validation` stays PENDING here.

`run.repro_fig3b` assembles the Task-9.1 capstone driver (`run_full_model`, every Increment 0-8 mechanism in one source-ordered per-MCS loop) into a seeded ensemble over the Sego-2022 Fig-3B scenario (5% initial infection, 0.3 mm / 1225-cell patch at paper scale) and compares the ensemble mean against Fig. 3B's digitized acceptance bands (`targets/fig3b.json`: value = the paper's ODE reference curve, lo/hi = its reported 50-replica spatial-model spread, IFN + downstream immune/signal observables flagged `soft: true` per the paper's own Sec. 3.1 note on where spatial-vs-ODE spread is largest). At the REDUCED scale this task's fast pytest test uses (`replicas=2, cells_per_side=15, steps=20, seed0=0` -- 225 epithelial cells over ~0.09 simulated days), the ensemble does not land in-band for any of the 12 mapped observables across every digitized checkpoint (`band_eval['passed']=False`); the population-count observables (uninfected/infected/dead cells) show the largest misses because the target's own trajectory for those moves the farthest over its 0.0-3.5-day checkpoint window (uninfected 1164 -> ~2 cells) while this reduced run only reaches t_days~0.092 and a ~5.4x smaller starting population -- a scale-and-duration mismatch, honestly reported, not a claim that any Increment 0-8 mechanism is individually miscalibrated (each was validated independently in its own increment). The soft-flagged systemic/signaling observables (type1_ifn, antibodies, nk_cells, cd8_t_cells, il10) land in-band at more checkpoints (5-7 of 8) than the hard-flagged population counts, mostly because their early- checkpoint target values are still small and their bands are widened 1.5x -- not because those subsystems are individually better-calibrated. THIS STUDY DOES NOT CLAIM FIG-3B IS REPRODUCED: the paper-scale 50- replica, 1225-cell, ~3.5-day ensemble (`repro_fig3b`'s own docstring documents this as `replicas=50, cells_per_side=35, steps~720`) is the Mac-mini Phase-B follow-up that will actually set the `reproduced` verdict for Fig 3B. No band in `targets/fig3b.json` was edited and no model constant was tuned toward a better-looking number.

Result

The soft-flagged observables (type1_ifn, antibodies, nk_cells, cd8_t_cells, il10) land in-band at MORE checkpoints (5-7 of 8) than the hard-flagged population counts (uninfected_cells, infected_cells, dead_cells: 0-2 of 8) -- not because those mechanisms are individually more correct, but because (a) `bands.SOFT_WIDEN_FACTOR=1.5` widens their bands, and (b) their target trajectories start near-zero and stay small through the target's early checkpoints, so the reduced run's near-t=0 flat-extrapolated value happens to fall inside those wider, still-small early bands more often. This is a run-duration/scale coincidence, not evidence the immune-signaling subsystem is better calibrated than the epithelial-population subsystem at this scale -- the honest read is that population counts (uninfected/infected/dead) expose the scale-and-duration mismatch most starkly because their target trajectories move the farthest (1164 -> ~2 uninfected cells) over the checkpoint window, while the ODE-driven systemic signals (apcs, antibodies) move on a slower, ODE-integrated timescale that a ~0.1-day snapshot cannot resolve either way.

FIG-3B IS NOT REPRODUCED AT THE REDUCED SCALE THIS TASK'S FAST TEST USES, AND THIS STUDY DOES NOT CLAIM IT IS: at replicas=2, cells_per_side=15, steps=20, seed0=0 (225 epithelial cells vs. the target's 1225; ~0.09 simulated days vs. the target's 0.0-3.5-day checkpoint window), band_eval['passed'] is False and 0 of 12 observables land in-band at every digitized checkpoint (see report.key_metrics for the full per-observable breakdown). This is expected at this scale -- a smaller population and a far shorter run duration than the digitized bands assume -- not evidence any wired mechanism is incorrect; every Increment 0-8 subsystem this driver assembles was independently validated in its own increment's study. THE PAPER-SCALE 50-REPLICA, 35x35-CELL (1225-cell), ~2-SIMULATED-DAY ENSEMBLE (documented in repro_fig3b's own docstring as the Mac-mini Phase-B follow-up) IS REQUIRED BEFORE ANY reproduced VERDICT FOR FIG 3B CAN BE SET. This study's own verdict stays documented, and conclusion_verdicts.biological_validation stays PENDING. No band was widened, no constant was tuned, and targets/fig3b.json was not edited to make any number in this report look better than it is.

Plan & assumptions

Key assumptions

  • REDUCED-SCALE REPORT, NOT PAPER-SCALE REPRODUCTION -- every band_eval number in this study.yaml comes from the exact `replicas=2, cells_per_side=15, steps=20, seed0=0` config this task's fast pytest test uses, run directly to produce these numbers honestly, NOT from any paper-scale (50-replica, 1225-cell) run. The Mac-mini Phase-B follow-up (documented in `repro_fig3b`'s own docstring) is the run that will set any `reproduced` verdict.
  • RUN-DURATION MISMATCH -- the reduced config's 20 records x `mcs_per_step=7` x `s_per_mcs=60` = 8400s = ~0.097 simulated days falls far short of `targets/fig3b.json`'s checkpoint window (0.0-3.5 days); `bands.series_in_band`'s flat extrapolation carries the model's near-t=0 value out to every later checkpoint, which mechanically drives most of the reported misses -- a documented property of this reduced config, not a bug in `bands.series_in_band` (whose extrapolation behavior is itself Task 9.0's, unmodified here).
  • POPULATION-SCALE MISMATCH -- `cells_per_side=15` gives 225 epithelial cells vs. `targets/fig3b.json`'s `scenario.total_epithelial_cells=1225` (`cells_per_side=35`); raw population counts (uninfected/infected/dead/ macrophages/nk/cd8) cannot match the target's absolute magnitudes at this reduced population even with perfectly calibrated rate constants.
  • INHERITED CALIBRATION-PENDING ITEMS from `run_full_model` (Task 9.1, unchanged here) -- the documented `NK_CD8_CHEMOTAXIS_ENGINE_SCALE=100x` chemotaxis engine-unit correction (Increment 7), and reduced-eta near-inert recruitment/nearby-killing subsystems flagged in Task 9.1's own docstring -- carried forward, not resolved by this task.
  • `targets/fig3b.json` WAS NOT EDITED and no model constant was tuned to narrow the reduced-scale gap reported here -- per the task brief's explicit instruction, a miss is reported honestly, not concealed.
