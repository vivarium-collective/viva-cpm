Q Adding macrophage-released chemokine and IL-10 diffusible fields (Task 6.1: `fields.add_chemokine_field`/`add_il10_field`, with per-cell secretion regulated by an IL-10-Hill self-regulation loop on macrophages and a `1-resist` gate on uninfected epithelial cells): does the chemokine field actually form a spatial GRADIENT centered on the macrophage cluster (the attractant signal Increment 7's NK/CD8+ cells will chemotax up), and is IL-10 genuinely positive and increasing from BOTH its regulated sources (macrophage, uninfected-cell)?

Claim

Wiring macrophage-released chemokine + IL-10 diffusible fields, with per-cell secretion regulated by a shared IL-10-Hill self-regulation loop (macrophages) and a `1-resist` gate (uninfected epithelial cells), produces a genuine chemokine GRADIENT centered on the macrophage cluster: per-cell, macrophages read 0.00261 vs. 0.00062 at a far uninfected epithelial patch (4.2x near/far), and a raw-lattice radial profile (position-independent) decays monotonically from 0.00195 at the macrophage-centered innermost bin to 0.00033 at the outermost of 6 bins (5.9x end-to-end drop). IL-10 is positive and increasing from BOTH its regulated sources (macrophages 2.97e-4, uninfected cells 3.71e-5, final step). This is a FIELD-MECHANISM validation claim (the gradient future NK/CD8+ recruitment, Increment 7, will chemotax up genuinely exists and is correctly centered) -- NOT a claim that any quantitative reproduction target is met, that `sig_1`'s true TNF-dependent dynamics are modeled (a documented constant stub is used, Increment 8), or that recruitment/ chemotaxis toward the field is wired (Increment 7). That fuller reproduction verdict remains PENDING until Increment 9 (the capstone).

Building on Increment 5's macrophage-localization mechanism, this study adds two macrophage-released diffusible fields -- chemokine (attractant for future NK/CD8+ recruitment, Increment 7) and IL-10 (an immunoregulatory cytokine) -- both secreted by macrophages under a shared IL-10-Hill self-regulation loop (`signaling.macrophage_secretion_scale`, each macrophage gated by its OWN local IL-10 reading), with uninfected epithelial cells contributing a second, independently-gated IL-10 source (`signaling.uninfected_il10_scale`, `1-resist`, resist from local type-I IFN). Over a 15-update run (seed=17, default scenario), the chemokine field forms a genuine spatial gradient centered on the macrophage cluster: 4.2x higher per-cell at macrophages than at a far uninfected epithelial patch, and a raw-lattice radial profile (independent of any cell's position) decays monotonically 5.9x from the macrophage-centered innermost bin outward across 6 bins. IL-10 is positive and increasing from both regulated sources (macrophages 2.97e-4, uninfected cells 3.71e-5), neither pinned at 0 nor saturating instantly. This demonstrates the FIELD mechanism behind the paper's chemokine-driven recruitment claim -- the gradient future NK/CD8+ chemotaxis (Increment 7) will follow genuinely exists and is correctly centered -- but nothing chemotaxes toward it yet, and several source terms are documented approximations, not reproductions: `sig_1` (the source's live TNF/global-ODE state gating both fields' secretion) is a fixed constant STUB, not the true dynamic value (Increment 8); `g_1`/`g_2`'s cellularization defaults to the 0.3mm-scenario `eta` (this scenario has no exact patch size of its own, Increment 8/9); global boundary secretion terms and IL-10's uniform initial condition are both deferred (no field-write API for the latter; Increment 8/9). No reproduction verdict against any figure is made -- that stays PENDING until Increment 9.

Test

Wire the chemokine + IL-10 fields (`fields.add_chemokine_field`/ `add_il10_field`, Task 6.1) and their per-cell secretion regulation (`signaling.macrophage_secretion_scale`/`uninfected_il10_scale`) into the Increment-5 non-confluent macrophage scenario, and drive `run.run_macrophage_signaling` (steps=15, seed=17, default scenario knobs) to measure: (a) the chemokine field's per-cell near (macrophages) vs. far (uninfected epithelial patch, `separation_sites` away) split, and its raw-lattice radial profile (6 concentric bins) around the macrophage- cluster centroid; (b) IL-10 positivity and growth at both the macrophage and uninfected-cell source populations. Evidence: `tests/ test_influenza_signaling.py`'s 4 integration tests (`test_chemokine_and_il10_fields_are_positive_after_macrophage_signaling_run`, `test_chemokine_field_is_highest_near_macrophages_and_decays_with_distance`, `test_il10_positive_from_macrophage_and_uninfected_sources`, `test_macrophage_signaling_run_is_deterministic`) plus the full localization numbers recorded in task-6.1-report.md. A minimal in-package figure (`pbg_cpm_studies/influenza/viz.py::signaling_fields_figure`, Task 6.2) renders the chemokine radial profile (panel a) and the IL-10 trajectories at both source populations (panel b).

Model signaling-fields
Measures `run.run_macrophage_signaling`'s lattice-sum chemokine and IL-10 totals must both be positive and strictly greater than their (zero) starting values by the final step. ·Per-cell chemokine at macrophages must exceed chemokine at the far uninfected epithelial patch, AND the raw-lattice radial profile around the macrophage-cluster centroid must be positive in the innermost bin, higher in the innermost than the outermost bin, and predominantly non-increasing with distance (at most one local bump allowed, from noise/geometry). ·`il10_at_macrophages` and `il10_at_uninfected` must both be positive at the final step -- confirming both regulated secretion pathways (macrophage Hill self-regulation, uninfected 1-resist gating) actually engage. ·Two `run.run_macrophage_signaling(steps=8, seed=17)` calls must produce identical `total_chemokine`, `total_il10`, and `chemo_radial_profile` series -- no unseeded randomness in the field mechanism.

Result

The chemokine gradient is measured TWO independent ways that agree: a simple per-cell near/far comparison (macrophages vs. a far epithelial patch, 4.2x) and a raw-lattice radial profile around the macrophage centroid that is fully independent of cell positions (5.9x, 6 bins, predominantly monotonic decay). Agreement between a per-cell reading and a position-independent lattice reading rules out the gradient being an artifact of which cells happen to sample which lattice sites. IL-10's dual-source regulation (macrophage Hill self-regulation AND uninfected `1-resist` gating) both engage in the same short run, using the SAME IL-10-Hill parameters that gate chemokine secretion (source: `b_c`/`b_l` share `sig_1`/`g_1`/`g_2`/`d_2`) -- a single shared regulatory loop driving two distinct fields, matching the source's structure even though `sig_1` itself is a documented stub (see caveats).

Conclusion

CHEMOKINE/IL-10 FIELD MECHANISM DOCUMENTED, NOT REPRODUCED. The chemokine field forms a genuine spatial gradient centered on the macrophage cluster -- 4.2x higher per-cell at macrophages than at a far uninfected epithelial patch, and a position-independent raw-lattice radial profile decaying monotonically 5.9x from the innermost (macrophage-centered) bin to the outermost of 6 -- confirmed two independent ways that agree. IL-10 is positive and increasing from BOTH its regulated sources (macrophages 2.97e-4, Hill self-regulated; uninfected cells 3.71e-5, 1-resist gated), sharing the same underlying Hill-constant plumbing that also gates chemokine secretion. Both fields' lattice-wide totals grow monotonically and stably over the run. This validates the FIELD mechanism (macrophage- released chemokine/IL-10, spatially localized, positive and regulated) -- it is NOT a claim that any quantitative reproduction target is met, that the true TNF-dependent sig_1 dynamics are modeled (a documented constant stub is used, deferred to Increment 8), that g_1/g_2's scenario-exact cellularization is used (the 0.3mm-scenario eta approximation is used instead, Increment 8/9), that global boundary secretion terms or IL-10's uniform initial condition are modeled (both deferred, Increment 8/9), or that anything chemotaxes toward the new chemokine gradient yet (Increment 7's scope). No Figs 2/3A-adjacent reproduction claim is made -- that verdict stays PENDING until Increment 9 (the capstone).

Plan & assumptions

Key assumptions

  • SIG_1 STUBBED — the source's `sig_1` (`Sigma1 := a_11*T + a_12*D`) is a live TNF-dependent global-ODE-solver state, not a fixed literal; Task 6.1 introduces a SEPARATE, documented `il10.sig_1_stub` constant (distinct from Task 6.0's own honest non-numeric `il10.sig_1` documentation string) chosen so the Hill ratio term sits at a genuine non-degenerate self-regulation point (Hill(0)~=0.975 sliding toward Hill(large L)~=0.5) rather than trivially saturating. The L-dependent self-regulation SHAPE and the chemokine gradient's spatial shape both hold regardless of the stub value; the true TNF-dependent DYNAMICS are deferred to Increment 8.
  • G_1/G_2 ETA APPROXIMATION — `il10.g_1`/`g_2`/`d_2`'s cellularization (`fields.il10_hill_constants`) defaults to the 0.3mm-scenario `eta` (`scaling.eta_0p3mm`), the package's existing convention for a scenario (the non-confluent macrophage scenario) with no exact patch size of its own. Order-of-magnitude, documented, not scenario-calibrated (Increment 8/9).
  • GLOBAL BOUNDARY SECRETION + IL-10 IC DEFERRED — the source's `num_macro`-scaled global boundary terms are not modeled (Increment 8); IL-10's uniform initial condition is skipped because the engine has no field-write API (fields build up from sources only, starting at 0; the formula and raw `b_lh` constant are already recorded in `params.yaml` by Task 6.0) (Increment 9).
  • NO CHEMOTAXIS ON THE NEW FIELDS YET — this study measures that the chemokine gradient EXISTS and is correctly centered; wiring NK/CD8+ recruitment to chemotax up it is Increment 7's scope, not modeled here.
  • Absolute chemokine/IL-10 magnitudes are NOT calibrated against the paper's figures (Increment-9 territory) — the fidelity criterion tested here (gradient shape centered on the macrophages, dual-source IL-10 positivity) holds cleanly, not the exact numeric magnitudes.
  • Same 2D-approximation geometry and modest-scale scenario knobs as Increment 5's macrophage-response study (non-confluent 2D patch, both clusters domain-interior, 6 macrophages, 1 infected cell) — see that study's key_assumptions for the full geometry rationale, unchanged here.
