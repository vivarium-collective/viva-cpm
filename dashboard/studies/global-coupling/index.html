Claim

The hybrid Price-2015 global ODE (10 systemic species, discrepancy #9) integrates stably once per MCS and responds to the spatial infection. All three stubs carried since Increments 5-7 are RESOLVED as dynamic, source-faithful mechanisms: dynamic sig_1 (`a_11*T + a_12*D`, the unchanged Michaelis secretion form, discrepancy #11), ODE-driven recruitment (chemokine/APC Hill inflows with the source's CD8-no- baseline asymmetry preserved, discrepancy #12), and NK/CD8 killing extended with a well-mixed NEARBY term (verified via a documented test seam) alongside the unchanged Increment-7 LOCAL contact term. THIS STUDY DOES NOT CLAIM THESE MECHANISMS ARE ALREADY CALIBRATED — at this driver's default reduced-scale patch (eta ~1.4e-4), dynamic sig_1's value is ~6 orders of magnitude below the retired static stub (2.44), collapsing the secretion-scale factor to near-zero; ODE-driven recruitment produces sub-0.01-per-MCS rates (no observable integer population growth from the chemokine/APC signal within a short run, only from the baseline term at a boosted eta); and the nearby-killing term, grown via natural recruitment alone over 300 MCS, remains orders of magnitude too weak to fire. These are FIELD-vs-ODE UNIT-SCALE CALIBRATION gaps — the same root-cause category as Increment 7's chemotaxis-engine-scale correction — deliberately reported as open, NOT tuned away, and deferred to Increment 9.

The Price (2015)-derived global (whole-organ) immune ODE compartment — 10 systemic species `{NB, N, T, X, A, B, P, W, G, O}` (neutrophils blood/tissue, TNF, ROS, antibody, IL-12, APC, IL-12, IFN-gamma, CD4+ Th1) — now integrates once per MCS (scipy LSODA, dt=60s, approximating the source's RoadRunner CVODE integrator), fed each MCS by the spatial model's cell counts, field integrals, and resistance-weighted infected- load terms (discrepancy #9's hybrid correction: only these 10 states are advanced by the integrator, the spatialized species are read as inputs). The freshly-integrated TNF, combined with the spatial dead-epithelial count, now drives a DYNAMIC macrophage chemokine/IL-10 secretion-scale factor (`sig_1 = a_11*T + a_12*D`, the unchanged Increment-6 Michaelis form, discrepancy #11) in place of the static Increment-6 stub; ODE- driven recruitment (discrepancy #12) activates/removes CPM macrophage/ NK/CD8+ cells from a pre-seeded dormant reserve pool via chemokine- and APC-Hill-driven inflow (CD8+ strictly APC-driven with NO homeostatic baseline, unlike macrophage/NK, per the source's own asymmetry); and NK/CD8 cytotoxic killing now combines the Increment-7 LOCAL contact term with a well-mixed NEARBY-population term driven by ODE surrogates (`K_nb`/`E_nb`). Every one of these three mechanisms is individually verified correct: sig_1 is genuinely time-varying and monotone-rising with TNF; CD8+'s no-baseline asymmetry is preserved exactly (inflow == 0.0 with the signal zeroed); and the nearby-killing term clears infected cells when a sufficiently large nearby population is present (via a documented test seam). This study's central, deliberately UN-hidden finding is that at this driver's small reduced-scale default (eta ~1.4e-4), none of these mechanisms' MAGNITUDES yet reconcile with the values they replaced or need to matter within a feasible run: dynamic sig_1 is ~6 orders of magnitude below the retired static stub (2.44), collapsing macrophage secretion regulation to near-zero; ODE-driven recruitment produces sub-0.01-per-MCS chemokine/APC-signal-driven rates (no observable integer population growth there; a boosted eta DOES seed cells, but from the homeostatic baseline, not the signal); and the nearby-killing term, grown via natural recruitment alone over 300 MCS, remains orders of magnitude too weak to fire. These are FIELD-vs-ODE UNIT-SCALE CALIBRATION gaps -- the same root-cause category already recorded for macrophage/NK/CD8 chemotaxis in Increments 5 and 7 -- now propagating into the ODE-coupling layer this task adds, deliberately reported as open Increment-9 calibration items rather than tuned away. Discrepancy #9 (hybrid ODE), #10 (spatial-string scalings), #7 (resist- direct, carried), LSODA-vs-CVODE, the reserve-pool recruitment-geometry approximation, and 2D-approximation geometry are all additional open items, not resolved here.

Result

Every one of this task's three "resolved" mechanisms hits the SAME root cause the investigation has flagged repeatedly since Increment 5: this engine's small default-scale patch produces field concentrations and population counts many orders of magnitude below what the source's literal ODE constants (calibrated at `tot_ec_ODE=250000`, the paper's full 1mm^2 organ scale) were designed to consume. `eta` (= `num_epithelial / 250000`) is tiny (~1.4e-4) at this driver's default `side=30`, so every `eta`-scaled coupling term — the cellularized Hill-dissociation constants driving recruitment, the nearby-population surrogates driving the new killing term, and (via `a_11`/`a_12`) the dynamic sig_1 itself — collapses toward zero long before it can produce an observable integer effect within a feasible step budget. This is NOT a new failure mode this task introduced; it is the field-magnitude/ engine-unit-scale gap already recorded in `cytotoxic-killing`'s `main_insight` and `macrophage-response`'s localization numbers, now propagating into the ODE-coupling layer this task adds on top. Increment 9's capstone calibration needs to treat sig_1, recruitment, and nearby-killing as one COMBINED eta-scale calibration target, not three separate ad hoc corrections.

Plan & assumptions

Key assumptions

  • DISCREPANCY #9 (hybrid ODE, load-bearing) -- only the 10 systemic/integrated states {NB, N, T, X, A, B, P, W, G, O} are advanced by the scipy solve_ivp integrator each MCS; the spatialized species {H, I, M, E, K, L, C, F, V, DH} are read from the CPM world each MCS as ODE inputs, never integrated -- per the source's own spatial-coupling model variant, NOT the full ~20-variable well-mixed ODE string.
  • DISCREPANCY #10 (spatial-string scalings) -- cellularized constants use the source's documented s_t/s_v/s_l (time/population/density) scale-token form from the spatial-model variant of the immune-model string, a judgment call the Task 8.1 brief mandated over the well-mixed variant's alternative scaling.
  • DISCREPANCY #11 (sig_1 dynamic form, functional form unchanged) -- sig_1 = a_11*T + a_12*D and the Michaelis secretion-scale function are implemented verbatim from Increment 6; the ~6-orders-of-magnitude MAGNITUDE gap vs the retired static stub is a calibration item at this reduced scale, not a functional-form deviation.
  • DISCREPANCY #12 (recruitment asymmetry, implemented literally) -- CD8+ inflow is strictly APC(P)-Hill-driven with NO homeostatic baseline term, unlike macrophage/NK which keep a positive baseline even with their chemokine signal zeroed; implemented per the source, not symmetrized for convenience.
  • DISCREPANCY #7 (resist-DIRECT), carried from Increment 7 -- both the LOCAL and the new NEARBY killing terms multiply resist DIRECTLY, not (1-resist), per the source's literal ContactKillingSteppable form; not adjudicated as a bug or an intentional antigen-presentation effect.
  • LSODA approximates CVODE -- scipy.integrate.solve_ivp's LSODA method stands in for the source's RoadRunner/libRoadRunner CVODE integrator; not bit-for-bit identical, not expected to be.
  • RESERVE-POOL RECRUITMENT GEOMETRY (engine-imposed approximation) -- the engine's World.add_cell/seed_block errors after finalize, so recruited cells activate from a pre-seeded dormant reserve pool parked in the domain's Medium margins (an interior reservoir), not minted at the lesion as the source's new_immune_cell_by_type does; immaterial to this task's population-count observable, but a geometry difference worth flagging for Increment 9.
  • 2D-APPROXIMATION geometry, carried forward -- the same non-confluent interior-placed 2D patch as Increments 5-7, not the source's z=2 immune-layer-over-confluent-z=1-sheet geometry.
  • FIELD-vs-ODE UNIT-SCALE CALIBRATION not yet reconciled at reduced scale -- this study's central, three-part honest finding (sig_1's magnitude collapse, recruitment's sub-single-cell rates, nearby-killing's too-weak natural buildup), all tracing to the same tiny default eta (~1.4e-4); reported honestly as an open Increment-9 calibration target, not tuned away by adjusting a_11/a_12, the Hill dissociation constants, or the killing-rate divisor until the numbers look better.
  • No Figs 3B/5/7 quantitative reproduction claim exists yet -- that is Increment 9, the capstone, which needs this study's calibration gaps resolved first.
