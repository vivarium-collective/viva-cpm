Q With every mechanism subsystem from Increments 0-8 wired into ONE per-MCS loop (`run.run_full_model`, Task 9.1), does sweeping that model over Sego-2022 Fig-7's four initial-infection-fraction scenarios (0.1%, 0.5%, 1%, 5% of the epithelial patch pre-infected, zero initial virus field) reproduce the paper's central Fig-7/Sec-3.4 finding -- a threshold-like severity response in which every replica is non-lethal (the patch recovers) for fraction <=1%, even though the ODE model is fatal for all of these, while at fraction=5% the spatial model becomes comparably severe to the ODE model (though some replicas still retain surviving uninfected cells) -- and, honestly, does a REDUCED-scale sweep (the only scale a fast CI test can afford) show a consistent (non-more- surviving-at-higher-fraction) direction even though it cannot match the digitized bands' absolute magnitudes at paper scale?

Claim

`repro_fig7` is wired correctly: it sweeps a seeded ensemble of the full model across Fig-7's infection-fraction scenarios and evaluates each fraction against only its own acceptance-band subset. Fig-7 is not reproduced at reduced scale — per-fraction band containment fails, and the smallest tested fraction degenerates to a no-infection control since it rounds to zero seeded cells. The monotone dose-response direction still holds; a paper-scale ensemble is required before reproduction.

`run.repro_fig7` sweeps the Task-9.1 capstone driver (`run_full_model`, every Increment 0-8 mechanism in one source-ordered per-MCS loop) over Sego-2022 Fig-7's initial-infection-fraction scenarios (a fixed count of pre-infected epithelial cells, `round(frac*tot_cell)`, at 0.1%/0.5%/1%/5% of the patch, zero initial virus field, at 10000 epithelial cells / 20 replicas at paper scale) and compares each fraction's ensemble mean against that fraction's own digitized acceptance-band subset (`targets/fig7.json`, filtered per fraction by the scenario-grouping guard `_fig7_target_subset` -- now built on a shared `_scenario_target_subset` helper generalized from `repro_fig5`'s Task-9.0-review-flagged guard, verified not to change fig5's prior behavior). Result

The reduced-scale result here is dominated by a DIFFERENT mechanism than `repro_fig5`'s reduced-scale report: not a virus-field-units mismatch (extracellular_virus is actually FULLY in-band, 6/6, at frac=0.05, and 5/6 at frac=0.001 -- unlike fig5's ~1200x worst_miss for the same observable at both tested loads), but a POPULATION-SCALE ROUNDING ARTIFACT specific to `init_infection_frac` scenarios at small `cells_per_side`: frac=0.001 * 144 cells rounds to 0 pre-infected cells, so that "run" never seeds any infection at all, trivially landing uninfected_cells at 144/144 for all 15 records -- a value nowhere near fig7.json's frac=0.001 band (which expects infection to emerge and recede, e.g. t_days=6: lo=2000,hi=9000 at paper scale, or as a fraction of 10000 cells ~0.2-0.9, vs this run's degenerate 1.0 constant), hence uninfected_cells' 0/6 in-band and worst_miss=2.29 (the largest relative miss of any observable/fraction pair in this report). frac=0.05's 7 initially-infected-of-144 cells is a real (non-degenerate) seed, and its extracellular_virus/antibodies land fully in-band while uninfected_cells/infected_cells do not -- consistent with the same short-duration (~0.068 vs up to 15 simulated days) and population-scale (144 vs 10000 cells) mismatch `repro_fig5`'s study already documented for cell-count observables, which don't rescale cleanly with a smaller population the way field-integral/ODE-state observables sometimes land inside a wide digitized band by coincidence. The honest read: the DIRECTION of the fraction-response (more initially infected cells -> fewer surviving uninfected cells) is correctly reproduced by the wired mechanism at frac=0.05, but frac=0.001's reduced-scale result reflects a rounding degeneracy, not the mechanism's fidelity at that specific fraction -- a distinct, additional honesty caveat beyond `repro_fig5`'s scale/duration findings. UNITS FIX CONFIRMATION (Task-9.3-review MUST-FIX, this update): this study's own prior reasoning above is now CONFIRMED correct by direct re-measurement. `repro_fig5`'s ~1200x extracellular_virus miss was diagnosed and fixed as a structural raw-sum-vs-mean units bug in the repro-observable mapping layer (`_map_full_model_observables`, `viva_cpm_studies/influenza/run.py`; see `repro-fig5-viral-load`'s F-04) -- the same fix now applies to this study's `_FIG5_OBSERVABLE_MAP`-based mapping too (fig7 reuses that map). Re-running this study's exact reduced config (`fracs=(0.001,0.05), replicas=1, cells_per_side=12, steps=15, seed0=0`) after the fix gives BYTE-IDENTICAL extracellular_virus n_in/worst_miss to the pre-fix numbers reported above (5/6 worst_miss 0.33 at frac=0.001; 6/6 worst_miss 0.0 at frac=0.05) -- confirming this study's own earlier read was right: `init_infection_frac` scenarios (fig3b, fig7) never triggered the bug at reduced scale because the virus field here builds up gradually via secretion from a handful of infected cells rather than starting from a large uniform-load initial condition (only `init_viral_load` scenarios, i.e. fig5, seed a large enough raw field sum for the sites-factor to dominate at this reduced scale).

Plan & assumptions

Key assumptions

  • REDUCED-SCALE REPORT, NOT PAPER-SCALE REPRODUCTION -- every band_eval number in this study.yaml comes from the exact `fracs=(0.001,0.05), replicas=1, cells_per_side=12, steps=15, seed0=0` config this task's fast pytest test uses, run directly to produce these numbers honestly, NOT from any paper-scale (20-replica, all-4-fraction, 10000-cell) run. The Mac-mini Phase-B follow-up (documented in `repro_fig7`'s own docstring) is the run that will set any `reproduced` verdict.
  • ONLY 2 OF 4 FRACTIONS SWEPT AT REDUCED SCALE -- `fracs=(0.001,0.05)`, chosen to keep the reduced test's fast dose-response assertion meaningful (the largest available fraction-ratio contrast in fig7.json's own scenario list) without paying for a 4-fraction sweep in the fast test suite; fractions 0.005/0.01 are exercised only by `repro_fig7`'s default signature (`fracs=(0.001,0.005,0.01,0.05)`), not by this study's reported numbers, and are NOT swept at paper scale until the Mac-mini Phase-B follow-up.
  • FRAC=0.001 ROUNDING DEGENERACY AT REDUCED POPULATION -- `round(0.001 * 144) = 0`: the smallest tested fraction seeds ZERO pre-infected cells at `cells_per_side=12`, so that run is a no-infection control, not a real exercise of fig7.json's frac=0.001 scenario. At paper scale (`cells_per_side=35`, 1225 cells at the fig3b-matched 0.3mm patch, or 10000 cells at fig7.json's own 1.0mm patch scenario), `round(0.001*1225)=1` / `round(0.001*10000)=10` -- non-degenerate. This is specific to this reduced test config, not a driver defect, and is reported honestly rather than concealed.
  • RUN-DURATION MISMATCH -- the reduced config's 15 records x `mcs_per_step=7` x `s_per_mcs=60` = 6300s = ~0.068 simulated days falls far short of `targets/fig7.json`'s checkpoint window (0.0-15.0 days); `bands.series_in_band`'s flat extrapolation carries the model's near-t=0 (or, for frac=0.001, degenerate-constant) value out to every later checkpoint, which mechanically drives most of the reported misses -- a documented property of this reduced config, not a bug in `bands.series_in_band` (Task 9.0's, unmodified here).
  • POPULATION-SCALE MISMATCH -- `cells_per_side=12` gives 144 epithelial cells vs. `targets/fig7.json`'s `scenario.total_epithelial_cells=10000` (`cells_per_side=35`, `patch_mm=1.0` at paper scale); raw population counts (uninfected/infected cells) cannot match the target's absolute magnitudes at this reduced population even with perfectly calibrated rate constants.
