Q Introducing the macrophage cell type (`types.M`) into the CPM world and wiring it to chemotax up the extracellular-virus gradient (the engine's existing `World.set_chemotaxis` primitive, no Rust change): does the macrophage population actually LOCALIZE to the infection over time (mean centre-of-mass distance to the infected-cell centroid decreases), robustly across multiple seeds and with an UNBIASED (interior-placed, not wall-pinned) lambda=0 control, so the effect isn't a seed-specific curve-fit or a wall-drift confound?

Claim

Wiring macrophage chemotaxis to the (unchanged) virus field via the engine's existing linear-lambda primitive produces genuine spatial LOCALIZATION, robust across seeds and against a geometrically unbiased control: with both the macrophage cluster and the infection placed in the domain interior (start distance 47.20 sites, identical across seeds), chemotaxis-on reduces the macrophages' mean distance to the infection centroid in EVERY one of 5 tried seeds (mean change -13.39 sites, range -11.52 to -17.75), while the SAME starting configuration with chemotaxis off (lambda=0 control) shows a small, directionless change (mean -0.85, range -4.99 to +3.45) -- near-isotropic, not a masked drift. Chemotaxis-on beats the lambda=0 control in every individual seed (paired comparison), and the mean on/off gap (-12.54) exceeds the control's own seed-to-seed spread (8.44), so the effect is not explainable by control noise alone. This is a LOCALIZATION-MECHANISM validation claim (macrophages chemotax toward the infection, matching Fig 2B/3A's qualitative direction, and now demonstrated to replicate rather than asserted from a single seed), NOT a claim that any quantitative reproduction target is met, that macrophages phagocytose virus (the CC3D source has no such per-macrophage mechanism), or that recruitment dynamics are modeled (a fixed population is placed at scenario build time) -- that fuller reproduction verdict remains PENDING until Increment 9 (the capstone). The 5-seed ensemble is a modest sample, not a full statistical power analysis or source-scale replicate count (see caveats).

Macrophages (`types.M`) and a seeded epithelial infection are both placed in the domain's INTERIOR (each at least `margin_sites` from every noflux wall, separated by `separation_sites` of open Medium -- neither cluster wall-pinned, a geometry fix, Task 5.1 commit `4092d7c`, that removes a control-drift confound an earlier corner-placed version had), and macrophages are wired to chemotax up the extracellular-virus gradient using the engine's existing linear-lambda chemotaxis primitive (`World.set_chemotaxis`) over the SAME virus field (secretion/diffusion/ decay from infected cells) validated in Increment 2 -- no new field, no Rust change. Over a 40-update run, tested across 5 seeds (5, 17, 23, 42, 100) with fixed, non-tuned knobs, the macrophage population's mean distance to the infection centroid falls consistently when chemotaxis is on (mean change -13.39 sites, negative in 5/5 seeds), while the identical starting configuration with chemotaxis mechanically disabled (lambda=0) shows a small, directionless change (mean -0.85, no consistent sign across seeds) -- a genuinely near-isotropic control, not a masked drift. Chemotaxis-on beats the lambda=0 control in every individual seed, and the mean on/off gap exceeds the control's own seed-to-seed spread, so the effect is not explainable by control noise alone. A minimal 2-cell engine-sanity check isolates the same contrast even more sharply from the fuller scenario's multi-cell stochasticity. This demonstrates the qualitative mechanism behind Sego 2022's Fig 2B / Fig 3A macrophage- accumulation claim -- macrophages find and cluster at the infection, robustly across seeds -- but macrophages here ONLY localize: there is no virus phagocytosis (the CC3D source itself has none, only a global ODE clearance term deferred to Increment 8), no ongoing recruitment (a fixed 6-macrophage population is placed at scenario build time; chemokine/ APC-Hill-driven recruitment arrives in Increment 6/8), and the engine's linear chemotaxis form differs from the source's saturating/COM-scaled form. The 5-seed ensemble is a modest sample size, not a full statistical power analysis; the source's z=2 immune-layer geometry is still approximated here by a non-confluent 2D patch. All caveats are open Increment-9 items, not resolved here.

Test

Wire the macrophage cell type (`types.M`, Task 5.0) and virus-field chemotaxis (`immune.set_macrophage_chemotaxis`, Task 5.1) into a non-confluent 2D-approximation scenario (`immune.build_macrophage_scenario_spec`, fix round 1: both clusters interior-placed) and driver (`run.run_macrophage_response`, fix round 1: fixed non-tuned knobs), and measure the resulting `mean_distance_to_infection` series with chemotaxis on (lambda=5000, `params.yaml` default) vs off (lambda=0 control), across 5 seeds (5, 17, 23, 42, 100) (Task 5.1's integration test `tests/test_influenza_macrophage.py::test_macrophages_localize_to_infection_across_seeds`, which asserts ON is negative in every seed, ON < OFF in every seed, and the mean on/off gap exceeds OFF's own seed-to-seed spread; plus the minimal 2-cell engine-sanity tests, and the fuller localization-number table recorded in task-5.1-report.md's fix-round-1 section). A minimal in-package figure (`pbg_cpm_studies/influenza/viz.py::macrophage_response_figure`, Task 5.2) renders the mean-distance-to-infection trajectory (on vs off overlay) plus the macrophage centre-of-mass path for a single representative run.

Result

Fix round 1 (Task 5.1, commit `4092d7c`) addressed two rigor findings in the ORIGINAL single-seed/corner-placed demonstration: (1) a control-drift confound — a corner-pinned macrophage start meant the lambda=0 control's thermal/wall drift was NOT isotropic, it was biased to net-drift away from the corner, and that bias happened to point roughly toward the (centered) infection, inflating the apparent on/off gap; (2) single-seed/knob-tuning — all assertions and knob choices were made against seed=17 alone. The fix places BOTH clusters deep in the domain interior (no wall nearby, so the control has no preferred direction) and asserts across 5 seeds with knobs fixed in advance, not re-tuned per seed. The result is a STRONGER, more honest claim: the lambda=0 control is now genuinely close to isotropic (mean -0.85, fluctuating both signs, no directional bias), while chemotaxis-on is consistently and substantially negative in every seed — the on/off contrast is not a seed-specific curve-fit or a masked wall-drift artifact, it is a mechanism property that replicates. The absolute numbers are NOT comparable to the old single-seed corner-placement headline (the interior scenario starts at 47.20 sites vs. the old corner scenario's 26.302 sites — different geometry, different scale), but the qualitative and now-quantified finding is stronger: chemotaxis causes a consistent, seed-robust net approach that a fair (isotropic) control does not show.

Conclusion

LOCALIZATION MECHANISM VALIDATED, NOT REPRODUCED -- now demonstrated ROBUSTLY across seeds with an unbiased control (fix round 1, commit 4092d7c). Macrophages chemotaxing up the (unchanged, Increment-2) virus field via the engine's existing linear-lambda World.set_chemotaxis primitive genuinely localize to the infection: mean distance to the infection centroid decreases in 5/5 tried seeds (mean -13.39 sites, range -11.52 to -17.75) when chemotaxis is on, while the identical starting configuration (both clusters interior-placed, start distance 47.20 sites) with chemotaxis off shows a small, directionless change (mean -0.85, range -4.99 to +3.45) -- confirmed both by the full scenario (paired seed-by-seed comparison: on beats off in 5/5 seeds; mean gap -12.54 exceeds the control's own spread 8.44) and by a minimal, fully deterministic 2-cell engine-sanity check. This validates the LOCALIZATION mechanism (matching Sego 2022 Fig 2B / Fig 3A's qualitative direction) -- it is NOT a claim that any quantitative reproduction target is met, that macrophages phagocytose virus (the CC3D SOURCE has no per-macrophage virus-uptake mechanism -- only a global ODE clearance term, Increment 8 -- so the paper's Fig 2 "phagocytosis" panel is not reproduced here or claimed to be a source-literal mechanism), or that recruitment dynamics are modeled (a FIXED macrophage population is placed at scenario build time; chemokine/ APC-Hill-driven recruitment is Increment 6/8). The 5-seed ensemble is a modest sample -- enough to show the effect replicates and is not a seed-specific curve-fit, but not a systematic statistical power analysis or a source-scale replicate count (an open Increment-9 item). The scenario also uses a NON-CONFLUENT 2D approximation of the source's z=2 immune-layer-over-confluent-z=1-sheet geometry, and the engine's LINEAR chemotaxis form rather than the source's saturating/COM-scaled form -- both open Increment-9 items. No Figs 2/3A reproduction claim is made -- that verdict stays PENDING until Increment 9 (the capstone).

Plan & assumptions

Key assumptions

  • MODEST ENSEMBLE SIZE — 5 seeds (5, 17, 23, 42, 100), fixed non-tuned knobs (chosen before any seed's trajectory was inspected). This is enough to show the localization effect REPLICATES (5/5 seeds show chemotaxis-on beating the control; the mean gap exceeds the control's own spread) and is not a single-seed curve-fit, but it is not a systematic statistical power analysis, a formal significance test, or a source-scale replicate count. Increment 9 (full reproduction, at paper scale) is where a fuller statistical treatment belongs if the capstone needs it.
  • 2D-APPROXIMATION geometry — the CC3D source places immune cells in a z=2 layer above a CONFLUENT z=1 epithelial sheet, with macrophages seeded at high-virus MEDIUM sites; this study instead uses a NON-CONFLUENT 2D patch, with BOTH the epithelial/infection cluster and the macrophage cluster placed in the domain interior (open Medium around and between them) so macrophages have real lattice space to migrate through in-plane and the lambda=0 control is not wall-biased. Faithful z=2 immune-layer geometry is deferred to Increment 9.
  • NO virus phagocytosis — the CC3D SOURCE has no macrophage-attributed virus-uptake mechanism (only a global ODE clearance term, Increment 8); the paper's Fig 2 "phagocytosis" panel is therefore a paper-vs-source discrepancy, recorded honestly, not implemented here or in the source itself. Macrophages in this study ONLY localize (chemotax + adhesion + volume constraint).
  • RECRUITMENT is stubbed — a FIXED macrophage population (6 cells) is placed at scenario build time; ongoing chemokine/APC-Hill-driven recruitment (inflow/outflow) is not modeled here (Increment 6 for the chemokine field, Increment 8 for the APC-driven global-ODE coupling).
  • CHEMOTAXIS FUNCTIONAL-FORM gap — the source uses a saturating, per-cell-COM-scaled lambda (`chemotaxis_v_macro / (1 + concentration)`, `ChemotaxisSteppable.__update_chemotaxis_lambda`); the engine primitive used here (`World.set_chemotaxis`) is a fixed LINEAR-regime lambda per (field, type). The fidelity criterion tested here is the localization BEHAVIOR, not the exact functional form -- an Increment-9 calibration item, not fixed here.
  • Geometry fix (Task 5.1 fix round 1, commit `4092d7c`) — an earlier version of this scenario placed the macrophage cluster in a domain CORNER, diagonally opposite the (centered) infection. A noflux wall is a hard reflector on two sides at once at a corner, so that control's thermal/wall drift was NOT isotropic -- it was biased to net-drift away from the corner, which happened to point roughly toward the infection, inflating the apparent on/off gap. Placing BOTH clusters in the domain interior removes that bias. The ABSOLUTE distance numbers from that earlier version (26.302 -> 18.198 on, 26.302 -> 30.585 off, seed=17 only) are NOT comparable to this study's current numbers (different scenario geometry, different start distance) and have been fully superseded by the multi-seed interior-placement results above.
  • Domain/timing constants (30-site margin, 25-site separation, 3000-MCS field warmup, 40 x 10-MCS updates) are fixed, physically-motivated values (the field warmup approaches the virus field's own decay relaxation time, `1/decay_per_mcs ~ 3500` MCS) chosen BEFORE inspecting any seed's trajectory -- not re-tuned per seed, and not derived from a paper figure. Fine for mechanism validation; not a quantitative Fig-2B/3A distance-vs-time reproduction.
  • No chemokine/IL-10 field, no NK/CD8+ T cell, no NK/CD8 contact-killing, and no APC-driven global IFN/recruitment amplification exist yet -- all later increments.
